I build end-to-end machine learning systems — from data pipelines and model training through validation, containerization, and production integration. My work spans transformers, NLP on clinical data, and causal inference on longitudinal electronic health records.

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Aiomic in Copenhagen, where I build ML pipelines that combine transformers with classical machine learning for clinical data. Before that I completed a PhD in Machine Learning and Causal Inference at the University of Copenhagen, as a PhD Fellow in the PHAIR project at the Pioneer Centre for AI.

PhD thesis

Scalable Causal Inference on Electronic Health Records Using Transformers

University of Copenhagen, 2026

Transformer models trained from scratch on structured longitudinal electronic health records, fine-tuned for clinical prediction, and combined with causal-inference methods to estimate treatment effects from observational data.
