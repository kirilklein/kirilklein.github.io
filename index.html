Background

Research depth. Engineering ownership.

At Aiomic, I designed and built the ML system for clinical document analysis and brought it into production. It combines gradient boosting, fine-tuned encoder transformers and LLMs in one workflow, with blinded validation before deployment.

I completed my PhD at the University of Copenhagen and the Pioneer Centre for AI. I developed a method for estimating causal drug effects from the longitudinal records of more than 2 million patients, in PHAIR, a project involving the Danish Medicines Agency. My earlier training is in physics and computational physics.
